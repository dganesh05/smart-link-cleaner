Debugging Tips:
  • View content script logs: Open DevTools (F12) on this page, check Console
  • View background script logs: Go to brave://extensions/, click "service worker" under your extension
  • Check statistics: Click the extension icon in the toolbar
  • Force reload extension: Go to brave://extensions/, click reload icon on your extension
